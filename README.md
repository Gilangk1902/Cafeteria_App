## 🛒 Ordering Flow

### 1. Login
<img src="Images/order/login.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

Login as a customer to start ordering.

---

### 2. Browse Menu
<img src="Images/order/browse.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

Browse available food and drinks on their respective pages.

---

### 3. Select Item
<img src="Images/order/pick.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

Choose the food or drink you want and click **Buy** to add it to your cart.

---

### 4. Adjust Quantity
<img src="Images/order/quantity.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

Adjust the quantity of the selected item.  
You can return to the menu to add more items.

---

### 5. Checkout
<img src="Images/order/cart.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

Go to your cart and finalize your order.  
After clicking **Done**, the order will be sent to the kitchen.

## 👨‍🍳 Manage Orders (Admin)

🎥 Demo: https://youtu.be/6eh-iNVuO9s

### 1. Login as Admin
<img src="Images/manage order/login.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

Login using an admin account.

---

### 2. Navigate to Profile
<img src="Images/manage order/navigate.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

Go to the profile page.

---

### 3. View Orders
<img src="Images/manage order/view-order.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

Click **View Orders** to see incoming orders.

---

### 4. Order List
<img src="Images/manage order/order-list.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

A list of orders will be displayed.  
Select an order to manage.

---

### 5. Manage Order Status
<img src="Images/manage order/manage-order.png" width="200" style="margin-bottom: 10px;"/>

- Each item starts with **WAITING** status  
- When an item is ready, change its status to **DONE**

---

### 6. Complete Order

Once all items are marked as **DONE**, click **Done** to finalize the order.  
The order will then be removed from the list.
